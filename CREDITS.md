# Reference image credits

## Supplied CAD drawing portfolio

Ten distinct PNG exports were supplied by Chinunka Chabala for the drawing portfolio: five 13 Miles sheets, two Ovacado Estate sheets, one Chililabombwe mechanical sheet, one apartment HVAC sheet and one office HVAC sheet. The second upload of the office drawing is an exact duplicate and is included once.

Files in `assets/projects/` are exact copies of the supplied exports. Their original title blocks, credits, revision information and issue statuses remain visible in the full-sheet viewers. Preview windows and presentation artwork crop portions of the sheets; no linework or technical information is regenerated or retouched. Inclusion in this portfolio is not a claim of sole authorship, a direct client commission or completed installation. The repository's code licence does not grant rights to these drawings.

## Portfolio visual collection

The eleven CAD-based artworks in `assets/visuals/covers/`, `boards/` and `social/` embed crops of the supplied PNG exports in designed SVG layouts. Original linework is not regenerated. Presentation annotations are separate editorial overlays, not original equipment identifiers or design instructions. Original title blocks and drawing credits remain available on the full-sheet project pages. The original supplied logo remains unchanged.

The two images in `assets/visuals/concepts/` were created with the built-in image-generation tool for this portfolio. They depict an illustrative HVAC ceiling and a pumping/filtration plant room. They are not site photographs, verified engineering designs, exact CAD reconstructions or evidence of completed installation. These illustrations are used as decorative website imagery, separate from the CAD project records. Visible captions and embedded text were removed at the owner’s request. Generation prompts are recorded in `assets/visuals/PROMPTS.json`.
