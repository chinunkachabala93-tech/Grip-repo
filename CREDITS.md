# Reference image credits

## Supplied CAD drawing portfolio

Ten distinct PNG exports were supplied by Chinunka Chabala for the drawing portfolio: five 13 Miles sheets, two Ovacado Estate sheets, one Chililabombwe mechanical sheet, one apartment HVAC sheet and one office HVAC sheet. The second upload of the office drawing is an exact duplicate and is included once.

Files in `assets/projects/` are exact copies of the supplied exports. Their original title blocks, credits, revision information and issue statuses remain visible in the full-sheet viewers. Preview and detail windows use CSS to select portions of the sheets; no linework or technical information is regenerated or retouched. Inclusion in this portfolio is not a claim of sole authorship, a direct client commission or completed installation. The repository's code licence does not grant rights to these drawings.
