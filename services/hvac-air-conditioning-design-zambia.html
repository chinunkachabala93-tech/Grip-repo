Before we start

Is HVAC the same as air-conditioning?

HVAC stands for heating, ventilation and air-conditioning. An air-conditioning layout addresses cooling equipment and its connections; a broader building-services scope can also include ventilation and air distribution. The services and deliverables are agreed for each project.

Can I request drawings without installation work?

Yes. You can enquire about a defined design and CAD drawing scope. This page describes design and documentation services; any site or installation work needs a separately agreed scope.

What will the finished package contain?

Depending on your brief, it may contain equipment layouts, pipe and duct routes, schedules, selected installation details and supporting calculations. The deliverable list and revision process should be agreed before work begins.
