Before we start

Can one brief cover HVAC, water and sewer drawings?

Yes. A combined mechanical and plumbing brief can be discussed, with the deliverables for each discipline identified separately. This helps the project team coordinate service routes and review the package.

Does MEP coordination include electrical design?

MEP coordination involves electrical interfaces, but electrical design is a distinct responsibility. Mac Key Grip’s offering on this page is mechanical and plumbing design coordinated with the appointed electrical designer; confirm the required discipline responsibilities in the brief.

How does a coordinated drawing package help?

Showing service routes, equipment positions and relevant details together makes interfaces easier to review. Coordination depends on current drawings and information from the participating disciplines.
